06How it works

From store activity
to a considered next step.

Each stage adds context before it adds confidence. Explore the process.

01
OBSERVE

Start with what is available.

Bring together the authorized store information and operational signals that can inform an investigation.

VERIFY

Check the evidence first.

Confirm source records, date ranges, data coverage and freshness before interpreting a signal.

DETECT

Surface what may be changing.

Identify patterns and inconsistencies that are worth examining against the available context.

INVESTIGATE

Understand the possible reasons.

Examine related records and distinguish supported explanations from hypotheses and unknowns.

PRIORITIZE

Focus on what matters most.

Organize findings by relevance, significance and the strength of the available evidence.

ADVISE

Consider a clear next step.

Present a practical recommendation for the merchant to evaluate and decide whether to take.

MONITOR

Keep the context current.

Revisit relevant signals and identify when data freshness or coverage changes the picture.
