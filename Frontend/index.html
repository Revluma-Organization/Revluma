05 What Revluma investigates

A sharper view of the signals already in your store.

Revluma’s current focus is Shopify information it can access with merchant authorization. What can be investigated depends on granted access, sync coverage and data quality.

01

Revenue & order patterns

Examine available order records and changes in activity that may indicate potential revenue leakage. Signals need context and investigation — they are not proof of a loss or a recovery guarantee.

Orders
02

Product & catalog context

Use synchronized product information alongside business activity where available. Catalog coverage and stock context can vary; Revluma will not infer inventory history it does not have.

Products
03

Customer purchasing patterns

Explore patterns reflected in authorized customer and order records. This is not a promise of complete customer identity, intent or lifetime history.

Customers
04

Checkout records, where available

Review available checkout and abandoned-checkout records. Revluma does not claim full behavioral visibility into every checkout or diagnose friction without supporting evidence.

Checkouts
05

Data coverage & inconsistencies

Make sync status, time coverage and unavailable information part of the investigation, so missing context is not presented as a confident conclusion.

Integrity

Available sources, history and detail depend on Shopify permissions and successful synchronization. Revluma will identify limitations rather than imply complete coverage.

06 How it works

From store activity to a considered next step.

Each stage adds context before it adds confidence. Select a step to explore it, or follow the full sequence.

01 / OBSERVE

Start with Shopify information a merchant has authorized and that Revluma has successfully synchronized.

02 / VERIFY

Check the source, period, sync status and record consistency before relying on an apparent signal.

03 / DETECT

Surface changes or patterns in available information that may deserve a closer look.

04 / INVESTIGATE

Compare relevant records, examine context and distinguish evidence from possible explanations.

05 / PRIORITIZE

Organize findings by relevance and context — not by an unexplained score alone.

06 / ADVISE

Offer a clear, measurable next step for a merchant to consider and decide whether to take.

07 / MONITOR

Keep an eye on relevant signals and data freshness as new information becomes available.

Read-only intelligence · Decisions and actions stay with the merchant.

09 Trust starts with clarity

Useful intelligence.
Clear boundaries.

Revluma should make the limits of its view as clear as the insight itself.

01

Merchant-authorized access

Store information is accessed through a merchant-authorized Shopify connection. Available data depends on the permissions granted.

02

Read-only operation

Revluma investigates and recommends. It does not change prices or store settings, edit customer records, launch campaigns, send recovery messages or apply discounts.

03

Freshness is part of the finding

Sync coverage and data freshness can affect what can be concluded. Outdated or unavailable information should be identified, not quietly treated as current.

04

No outcome guarantees

Recommendations are there to inform your judgement. No revenue recovery or business outcome is guaranteed.

We do not publish security certifications or performance guarantees that have not been independently substantiated.
