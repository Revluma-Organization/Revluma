# Revluma public site — research & design direction

**Purpose:** synthesis for the public website rebuild. Reference pages were reviewed for their available live page content, composition and product storytelling. The research informed a new direction; it is not a component or visual replica exercise. Pacific Green Homes was examined for its immersive, video-led introduction; none of its footage or assets are used.

## Reference observations

| Reference | Useful principle for Revluma | What not to copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Linear | Tight typographic hierarchy, calm spacing and product UI that carries the explanation. | Dark purple gradients, issue-tracker vocabulary or Linear's exact visual system. |
| Ramp | Explain complex software in terms of the decision it helps someone make; use a clear, repeated conversion path. | Finance-specific claims, customer proof or high-velocity statistics Revluma cannot substantiate. |
| Attio | Make AI legible through believable interface moments and visible context. | Autonomous agent behavior, CRM workflows or implied write access. |
| Vercel | Crisp technical hierarchy, disciplined page rhythm and restrained use of dark space. | Infrastructure motifs, brand marks or developer-centric jargon. |
| Stripe | Translate complex capabilities into plain language and use purposeful transitions between content modes. | Stripe's gradient/wave treatment, payment metrics or unsupported proof points. |
| Triple Whale | Start from real commerce questions and the tension between fragmented systems and decision-making. | Its broad cross-channel data claims, ad platform integrations or automated actions. |
| Polar Analytics | Show how data context changes the quality of an answer; surface the relationship between business data and AI. | Its multi-source integrations and campaign execution, which are outside Revluma's current Shopify-only, read-only boundary. |
| Glean | Explain AI as a context problem and make trust and source coverage part of the product story. | Enterprise security badges, integrations and scale claims not independently substantiated for Revluma. |
| PostHog | Demonstrate an investigation as a sequence: question, evidence, reasoning, finding. Label illustrative data explicitly. | Product analytics integrations, code agents or automated implementation actions. |
| Shopify | Use direct merchant language and concrete commerce concepts; keep the first step uncomplicated. | Shopify's platform claims or presentation as if Revluma were a Shopify product. |
| Pacific Green Homes | Build an immediate sense of place with an immersive film, strong contrast and a restrained layer of information. | Its copyrighted video, imagery, layout and construction-brand expression. |

## Cohesive Revluma direction

- **Position:** AI operational intelligence for commerce — a Shopify intelligence layer that helps merchants know what is happening, understand why, prioritize and consider an action.
- **Narrative:** business signals are difficult to interpret → Revluma assembles relevant authorized context → Rev investigates a question → the Command Center makes findings reviewable → evidence and limitations remain visible → the merchant decides.
- **Visual system:** deep navy and ink for product/film moments; quiet cool paper for explanatory sections; a single Revluma blue for emphasis; functional status colors only where needed. DM Sans and a measured, editorial scale keep the interface approachable without feeling generic.
- **Composition:** full-bleed cinematic opening, then deliberate shifts between pale editorial sections and dark product demonstrations. Thin rules, small indices and interface-like evidence details provide structure without wrapping everything in rounded cards.
- **Motion:** the existing first-party commerce-operations film is loaded only after initial render on larger screens and only when reduced motion is not requested; mobile and reduced-motion visitors keep the optimized poster. The poster is the complete fallback. Scroll reveals are progressive enhancement; process and question demos are keyboard-operable and content remains present without animation.
- **Distinctly Revluma:** expose the boundary between verified facts, derived metrics, hypotheses and unknowns. Treat data coverage and freshness as part of the answer. Clearly label synthetic product demonstrations and keep all actions human-led.

## Product-claim guardrails used in the copy

The site limits scope to information available through merchant-authorized Shopify access and successful synchronization. It describes order/revenue patterns, product/catalog context, customer purchasing patterns, available checkout records and data coverage without promising complete behavioral visibility, full history, inventory movement or causal diagnosis. It does not promise recovered revenue or imply that Revluma changes prices, store configuration, customer records, discounts, campaigns or recovery messages.
